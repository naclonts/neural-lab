# Independent bounded literature check — 2026-10-31

I reread `literature-current.md` and independently checked the primary [SUA-Net article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44163-026-02073-7), published 29 August 2026, particularly equations 7–14 and the discussion of validation. Its equations 7–10 do specify averaging the squared feature channels and the feature channels separately, then spatially pooling both to construct their moment difference. This is a joint channel-and-window statistic, not the average of separate within-channel spatial variances. The paper specifies max normalization and a learned spatial mask, and explicitly reports a channel-preserving variance ablation without fully specifying its implementation in these equations. The previous note's conclusion that this is close prior art but not evidence of an exact match is supported. Its ultrasound data, pretrained architecture and task do not predict a CIFAR outcome; its image-wise BUSI validation can allow patient overlap, as the article itself acknowledges.

Interpretation of the frozen local gate also has intrinsic limits. Its input is normalized dispersion `max(q−mu²,0)/(q+1e-4)`, not raw variance: when moments are nonnegative and consistent it is bounded below 1, and a change in local mean or activation scale can change the descriptor. The epsilon breaks exact scale invariance near zero, and the clamp handles negative numerical moment differences. Thus a variance-versus-mean contrast evaluates these particular learned gated architectures and their optimization under the frozen schedule; it does not isolate an abstract, pure effect of variance or establish a universally better statistic. A learned coefficient may suppress, reverse, or saturate dependence on its descriptor. The constant and plain controls help interpret whether feature-dependent gating and gating in general are useful, but do not by themselves explain a mechanism.

Two paired seeds, a shared development split and adaptively chosen same-split horizons restrict inference even if every frozen criterion passes: this is a prespecified joint screen conditional on earlier development, not independent replication, a significance test, or proof of generalization. Failure of a criterion is likewise evidence about this finite setup, not proof that all local-variance gates fail. These considerations do not change the frozen jobs or their endpoints. No active outputs were inspected and no training or queue action was performed.
